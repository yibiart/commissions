FAQ

Quick answers (full details in the TOS tab)

How do I pay?

Upfront through a PayPal invoice in EUR. You can pay in full or split it into two parts: the first for the sketch and the second for the color.

Can I ask for changes?

Yes. You review the sketch and suggest changes. After you approve it I move on to coloring, where minor adjustments can still be made.

Can I get a refund?

Once I start drawing the sketch, refunds are not permitted.

Can I use the art commercially?

Only with prior agreement and a licence purchased from me. For book covers and commercial projects, email me for an accurate quote.

Can I share my commission?

Yes, for personal use, as long as you credit me as the artist.

What if I need something not listed?

Send me an email with your idea and I will let you know and quote it.
